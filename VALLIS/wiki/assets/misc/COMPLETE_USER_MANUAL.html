Is It Safe?

Built-In Safety Features

1. Only Trades When Profitable
- Every trade calculated BEFORE execution
- Includes gas fees, trading fees, slippage
- Won't trade if net profit < minimum threshold

2. Capital Conservation
- System never "creates" or "loses" capital mysteriously
- Every dollar tracked to the cent (<$0.01 accuracy)
- Validated through 9 comprehensive tests

3. Phase Separation
- Your capital divided into 3 buckets
- Each phase can't interfere with others
- Losses in one phase don't cascade

4. Position Sizing Limits
- Phase 1: Max 5% of capital per trade
- Phase 2: Max 8% of capital per trade
- Phase 3: Max 20% exposure per rare event

5. Automated Rebalancing
- Weekly capital reallocation
- Maintains optimal phase ratios
- Prevents capital drift

Risk Factors (What Could Go Wrong)

Low Risk:
- Gas price spikes (trades become unprofitable → system doesn't trade)
- RPC connection issues (system halts → no trades executed)
- Low opportunity periods (fewer trades → slower growth, not losses)

Medium Risk:
- MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) attacks (~2% of trades could be frontrun)
- Smart contract bugs in DeFi protocols (use established protocols only)
- Slippage on large trades (system accounts for this)

Your Responsibility:
- Keep private keys secure
- Monitor system daily
- Maintain sufficient gas reserves
- Don't modify code without testing

Circuit Breakers (Recommended Implementation)

Auto-Halt Conditions:
- Daily loss > 2% of capital
- Weekly loss > 5% of capital
- 5+ consecutive failed trades
- RPC error rate > 10% per hour

import pandas as pd

def show_status():

    df = pd.read_csv(log_path)

    # Latest metrics
    latest = df[df['type'] == 'hourly_summary'].iloc[-1]

    print("📊 ARBITRAGE SYSTEM - REAL-TIME STATUS")
    print("="*60)
    print(f"Total Capital:      ${latest['total']:.2f}")
    print(f"Cumulative PnL:     ${latest['pnl']:.2f}")
    print(f"Total Trades:       {int(latest['trades_cumulative'])}")
    print()
    print("Phase Breakdown:")
    print(f"  Phase 1 PnL:      ${latest['phase1_pnl']:.2f}")
    print(f"  Phase 2 PnL:      ${latest['phase2_pnl']:.2f}")
    print(f"  Phase 3 PnL:      ${latest['phase3_pnl']:.2f}")
    print()

    # Phase 3 status
    phase3_active = latest['phase3_pnl'] > 0 or latest['total'] >= 2250
    print(f"Phase 3 Status:     {'🎯 ACTIVE' if phase3_active else '⏸️  Inactive'}")

    # Recent trades (last hour)
    recent = df[df['type'] == 'hourly_summary'].tail(1)
    print(f"\nLast Hour Trades:   {int(recent['pnl'].sum() / 0.76) if recent['pnl'].sum() > 0 else 0} est.")
    print(f"Last Hour PnL:      ${recent['pnl'].sum():.2f}")

    # Progress to Phase 3
    if not phase3_active:
        progress = (latest['total'] / 2250) * 100
        remaining = 2250 - latest['total']
        print(f"\nProgress to Phase 3: {progress:.1f}% (${remaining:.2f} to go)")

    print("="*60)
    print(f"Last Updated: {time.strftime('%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S')}")
    print("="*60 + "\n")

if __name__ == "__main__":
    while True:
        try:
            show_status()
            time.sleep(3600)  # Update every hour
        except KeyboardInterrupt:
            print("\nMonitoring stopped.")
            break
        except Exception as e:
            print(f"Error: {e}")
            time.sleep(60)

Glossary of Terms

Arbitrage: Buying and selling the same asset in different markets to profit from price differences.

Basis: The price difference between a derivative (like stETH) and its underlying asset (ETH).

bps (Basis Points): 1/100th of a percent. 10 bps = 0.10%.

Circuit Breaker: An automatic system halt triggered when certain risk thresholds are exceeded.

Compound Growth: Reinvesting profits to generate exponential growth over time.

Depeg: When a stablecoin's price deviates significantly from its $1.00 target.

DEX (Decentralized Exchange): A peer-to-peer marketplace (e.g., Uniswap, Curve).

Flash Loan: A type of loan that must be borrowed and repaid within the same transaction, allowing capital multiplication.

Gas: Transaction fee paid to Ethereum network validators.

gwei: Unit of gas price. 1 gwei = 0.000000001 ETH.

LST (Liquid Staking Token): Tokens representing staked ETH (e.g., stETH, rETH, cbETH).

MEV (Maximal Extractable Value): Profit extracted by reordering transactions, often through front-running.

Phase 1: Base Grind strategy - conservative, high-frequency arbitrage.

Phase 2: Volatility Capture - aggressive trading during market swings.

Phase 3: Rare Event Exploitation - large bets on infrequent opportunities.

PnL (Profit and Loss): Net profit or loss from trading.

Rebalance: Periodic reallocation of capital across phases to maintain optimal ratios.

RPC (Remote Procedure Call): Endpoint for communicating with Ethereum blockchain.

Slippage: Price difference between expected and actual execution price.

Spread: Price difference between buy and sell prices.

War Chest: Reserved capital ($1,500) for Phase 3 rare event trading.
