CONCLAVE

Community Priority Vote

Conclave is a monthly prioritization ritual. Tokens influence which bundle is implemented first. Voting is non-binding: the founder may still ship stability, security, or compliance updates at any time.

Important: Conclave tokens only affect priority. They do not unlock content, change odds, or guarantee delivery.
