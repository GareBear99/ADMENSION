Runes

Daily States

Runes describe system state only. They are not buffs.
RuneMeaning
LimitCaps reached; further execution denied (Sentinel).
PauseRegime not net-positive; system idles (Sentinel).
GatePhase locked; access denied until criteria met (Sentinel).
ObservationTrial/demo state; record without escalation (EVE).
RecordLogs/receipts written; immutable entry (Scar).
RepetitionSustained filtered execution; density over hero trades (Scar).
ResetCycle rollover; state cleared where allowed (Sentinel).
Runes may appear beside logs, games, and bot status. They never instruct risk escalation.
